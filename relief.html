Relief

A milled digital watch face for Wear OS. Large time, deep pockets for your data, and a surface you choose.

Features

  • 8 complication slots: any data from any app on your watch
  • Built-in weather, UV index, chance of rain, today's high and low, or nothing at all in empty fields
  • 7 accent colours and 10 surfaces: brushed, sunburst, perlage, Côtes de Genève, carbon and more
  • Hour colour, plate tint and rim marks
  • 12 or 24 hours, following your watch
  • 5 always-on styles, dimmed to 30, 45 or 60 %
  • 36 languages

Requirements

A Wear OS 5 or newer watch, such as Samsung Galaxy Watch4 and later, Google Pixel Watch, or OnePlus Watch 2 and later.

Support

Email primowatch3s@gmail.com. Relief collects no data: see the privacy policy.
