Privacy policy

Applies to every Primowatches watch face on Google Play (Relief) and every Primowatches app for Garmin watches (Strength). Last updated 26 October 2026.

Summary

Our watch faces do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. They contain no code, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

What a watch face shows

Our watch faces display information your watch already has, such as time, date, battery level, steps, heart rate and weather. It is provided by Wear OS and by the apps you choose for each complication slot, and drawn on your watch only. It never leaves your watch through the watch face, and we never receive it.

Permissions

Our watch faces request no Android permissions. Access to data shown in complications is controlled by Wear OS and by the app that provides each complication, under that app's own privacy policy.

Settings

Your choices, such as colours and surfaces, are stored by Wear OS on your watch. We have no access to them.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads, purchases and reviews, under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only anonymous, aggregated statistics from Google Play Console, such as the number of installs.

Garmin apps

Our Garmin apps have no network access, no analytics and no advertising, and we receive nothing from them. Your workouts, programs and settings are stored on your watch. When you choose to save a workout to Garmin Connect, it becomes a Garmin activity that syncs through Garmin's own apps, under the Garmin Connect privacy policy. Settings you change in Garmin Connect or the Connect IQ Store app are sent by Garmin to your watch only.

Children

Our watch faces and apps collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
